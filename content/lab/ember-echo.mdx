## Problem

Care settings already collect signals such as ECG, SpO2, heart-rate variability, blood pressure, and the electronic record. This prototype asks whether those existing streams could be studied for earlier warning. It does not answer that question.

## What I built

A research page for the idea. It is parked: the host is still online, and it is not maintained as a clinical system. Role: design and engineering.

## Key design decisions

This portfolio states the limit first. The parked page has carried stronger wording about detection and timing. Those claims are not repeated here, because they are not validated.

## What is real and what is simulated

This is a research prototype. It is not a validated detector and not for clinical use. No timing window is claimed. Partnerships are not claimed.

Research demo. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use.
