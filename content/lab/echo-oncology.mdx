## Problem

Reviewing a surgical case means holding a plan, imaging, and status in one place. Eagle Eye Guided is a research demo of that review, built around a fictional teaching case rather than a patient.

## What I built

A guided web demo with pre-operative, intra-operative, and post-operative steps. The interface includes a rendered view, teaching labels, and scripted status. Role: design and engineering.

## Stack and architecture

The demo is hosted on Vercel. The application stack beyond that is still to be written up.

The flow on the page is:

1. Pre-op summary of a fictional case
2. Intra-op view with simulated sensing
3. Post-op review marked as a teaching story

## Key design decisions

The demo labels teaching figures in the interface itself, including where a number is a story rather than a measurement. It also states that contactless sensing is simulated.

## What is real and what is simulated

The public demo states that contactless sensing and acoustic vitals are simulated and investigational, that the case is not a patient, and that no hardware is connected. Nothing on this page is a clinical result.

Research demo. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use.
