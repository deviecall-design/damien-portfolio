## Problem

Australian electricians were quoting from printed plans, with manual calculations for labour and materials. ElectraScan is an AI estimating product aimed at that workflow.

## What I built

A software interface for generating a bill of materials and tracking order value in dollars rather than only in length. Pricing covers copper, cable, and petroleum-linked materials. Further detail is still to come.
